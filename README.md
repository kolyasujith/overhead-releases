# Overhead

A macOS menu bar app that shows the planes flying over you, live, as small 3D aircraft crossing your screen.

## Download

Get **Overhead-*.dmg** from the [latest downloads](https://github.com/kolyasujith/overhead-releases/releases/tag/updates), open it, and drag Overhead to Applications. The app updates itself after that.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

Flight data from [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol) (ODbL) and its volunteer receivers.
